# Newsletter format

## Header

- Cutoff date
- Actual research period
- Baseline or comparison edition

## 1. Three-minute overview

- Three-line summary of the week's most important changes
- Up to five highlights
- For each: what changed, why it matters to the profile, industry, geography, and role tags
- A short `지난 회차와 달라진 점`

## 2. Highlight details

Use commute-first progressive disclosure by default. Show only three short reader-facing elements before expansion: what changed, one decisive fact, and the most relevant role implication. Put the fuller analysis below a `심층분석 보기` control when the format supports interaction.

For each highlight include:

1. One-line conclusion
2. `무엇이 달라졌나`: compare the announcement or event with the prior process, product, operating model, or market practice. Do not merely restate that it happened.
3. `확인된 사실`: provide two or three concrete facts such as dates, metrics, named capabilities, organizations, products, or use cases when the source supports them.
4. `사업·업무 구조 해설`: trace the effect through the business model, customer value, operating process, system or data flow, and decision points that actually change.
5. `Codex의 해석`: state the inference and the evidence-to-conclusion reasoning. Do not repeat the facts.
6. `직무 영향`: for only the relevant roles, specify likely work, stakeholders, required capabilities or tools, and success metrics. Give distinct implications for BA, Consultant, and PM/PO rather than generic advice.
7. `내 커리어 활용 포인트`: label it as interview, portfolio, learning, and/or company analysis, then give one concrete example that can be used immediately. Personalize the recommendation without adding a separate `내 경험과 연결` section.
8. `데이터 분석 질문`: name a useful metric, comparison group, hypothesis, or additional dataset that would test the interpretation.
9. `근거 한계` only when material uncertainty exists; do not add an empty caution by habit.
10. Original title, publisher, publication or event date, and URL.

Each highlight should contain enough substance to explain the mechanism and career implication, while avoiding repeated background or padded prose. Prefer a clear 400–700 Korean-character analysis per highlight when the evidence supports it; exceed that only when the topic genuinely requires it.

## 3. Other materials

Include five to ten concise items when available:

- Title, source, date, URL, one-line summary
- Reason it did not enter the highlights
- Follow-up, inaccessible, abstract-only, or weak-evidence status

## 4. Role radar

Use exactly three sections:

- BA
- Consultant
- PM/PO

For each, show the week's emphasis, supporting sources, repeated job-posting signals when available, and one preparation priority.

## 5. Career actions

Provide no more than three actions across learning, company/case analysis, interview preparation, or portfolio work.

## 6. Cross-highlight synthesis

End the reader-facing analysis with:

- The common change connecting the highlights
- Three implications that matter most to the active career profile
- Any tension or counter-signal that prevents a simplistic conclusion

## 7. Logic quiz

Turn the current edition's evidence into three approachable multiple-choice questions. Train reasoning without requiring the reader to write a full argument.

1. `사실 찾기`: distinguish a directly verified fact from an interpretation or unsupported causal claim.
2. `숨은 전제 찾기`: identify the assumption needed to connect evidence to a conclusion.
3. `적절한 결론 고르기`: choose a calibrated conclusion that reflects both the signal and its evidence limits.

Give three clear choices per question, one best answer, and a short explanation that teaches why the other choices overstate, dismiss, or misuse the evidence. Keep the wording accessible, avoid trick questions, and vary the actual facts and answers by edition.

## Internal research-quality check

Check before delivery, but do not show this as a routine reader-facing section:

- Coverage period and any expansion
- Source mix and material concentration
- Claims cross-checked
- Inaccessible or partially inspected materials
- Topics to track next time

Surface only a limitation that could materially change how the user interprets a specific item. Keep that limitation with the item as `근거 한계`.

## Read-depth principle

The newsletter must remain useful at three depths:

1. Three-minute overview
2. Detailed highlight reading
3. Extended signals and evidence review

Do not duplicate the same paragraph across sections.
