# Source and verification policy

## Coverage

- Start with the most recent 7 days relative to the execution date.
- Expand to 30 days only when the qualified pool is insufficient.
- Separate publication date, last-updated date, event date, and conference date.
- Keep evergreen fundamentals outside the current-news highlights.

## Source types

Search a meaningful mix of:

1. Company newsrooms, product releases, IR materials, and customer cases
2. Government, public institutions, industry associations, and research institutions
3. Company technology and engineering blogs
4. Reputable domestic and international industry media
5. Public materials from consultancies and market-research organizations
6. Comparable job postings from target and adjacent companies
7. Official conference programs, sessions, recordings, slides, and proceedings
8. Personal blogs and communities only for discovery or practitioner context

## Query matrix

Combine the active profile across three axes in Korean and English:

- Industry: IT services, enterprise software, SaaS, manufacturing, mobility, B2B telecom, finance, fintech
- Role: BA, business analyst, solution consultant, management consultant, CRM consultant, PM, PO, product manager
- Theme: AI agent, CRM/ERP integration, requirements, process analysis, data governance, customer experience, KPI, UAT, automation, AX/DX, change management

Vary queries by company, business problem, solution category, job title, conference, session, case study, and geography. Do not rely on a fixed set of sites.

## Verification

- Inspect original sources for all highlights.
- For material numbers, market size, or performance claims, seek a primary source or an independent corroborating source.
- For each highlight, collect enough evidence to explain the prior state, the new change, and the operating mechanism. One thin announcement is not sufficient for a high-confidence deep analysis.
- When independent corroboration is unavailable, keep the item only if it remains highly relevant and clearly label the vendor, employer, or organizer perspective.
- Mark vendor-reported outcomes and promotional material.
- Treat a program title, search snippet, or abstract as limited evidence.
- When sources conflict, show both claims and defer judgment unless a stronger source resolves it.

## Deduplication and diversity

- Deduplicate by event or claim, not only by URL.
- Link meaningful later developments as `후속 업데이트`.
- Compare against the most recently delivered edition even when one or more calendar days have passed. An unchanged event stays excluded regardless of its URL or publication date.
- A newly found URL that only corroborates an existing event updates that event's evidence or verification status; it is not a new newsletter item.
- A real change in a function, metric, schedule, policy, customer outcome, or released artifact may return as a `후속 업데이트`, showing only what changed since the last delivery.
- A relevant older item discovered late is `새로 발견한 자료`, not `신규 발표`.
- Check whether one company, vendor, country, or source type dominates the highlights.
- Include international sources when useful, but do not fill a quota with weak material.

## Ranking

Rank candidates by:

1. Fit with the active industries
2. Fit with BA, Consultant, or PM/PO
3. Novelty or material follow-up value
4. Strength of evidence
5. Usefulness for learning, interviews, or portfolio work

Keep five highlights as a maximum target, not a minimum requirement.
