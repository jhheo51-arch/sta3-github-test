# Conference research policy

## What to search

- Official event and organizer pages
- Keynotes and session pages
- Official recordings and presentation decks
- Proceedings, white papers, and post-event summaries
- Speaker or employer follow-up posts that clarify the session

Prioritize enterprise AI agents, CRM, ERP, SaaS, data governance, customer experience, AX/DX, manufacturing and mobility, finance and fintech, and work relevant to BA, Consultant, or PM/PO.

## Required fields

- Conference name and geography
- Event date and material publication date
- Session title
- Speaker and organization
- Material type: program, abstract, video, slides, paper, or recap
- Verified claim or theme
- Related industry and role
- Original URL
- Inspection status

## Inspection status

- `전체 확인`: the relevant recording, deck, paper, or detailed recap was inspected
- `부분 확인`: only part of the material was available
- `세션 소개만 확인`: program title or abstract only
- `접근 실패`: source could not be opened

Never summarize detailed session conclusions from a title or abstract alone.

## Lifecycle

Track a conference item through:

1. Program or call-for-content announcement
2. Event occurrence
3. Recording, slides, paper, or recap publication

When a later stage becomes available, connect it as a follow-up rather than presenting it as an unrelated duplicate.
