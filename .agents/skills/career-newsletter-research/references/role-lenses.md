# Role lenses

## BA

Look for changes in:

- Business processes and requirements
- Data definition, quality, and governance
- CRM, ERP, and system integration
- User scenarios, acceptance criteria, UAT, and KPIs
- Traceability from business problem to system behavior

For job postings, extract responsibilities, required artifacts, tools, stakeholders, data expectations, and success measures.

## Consultant

Look for changes in:

- Client business problems and industry economics
- AI, AX, DX, and solution strategy
- Current-state diagnosis and target-state design
- Proposal logic, implementation roadmaps, and change management
- Stakeholder alignment and measurable business value

For job postings, extract industries served, consulting stages, solution knowledge, communication expectations, and value-measurement language.

## PM/PO

Look for changes in:

- Customer problem and product goal definition
- Prioritization and user journeys
- Product, adoption, retention, and operational metrics
- Human approval and accountability in AI features
- Collaboration across business, design, engineering, data, and operations

For job postings, extract product scope, decision rights, metric ownership, experimentation expectations, and cross-functional responsibilities.

## Cross-role interpretation

When one item applies to multiple roles, explain the distinct implication for each rather than copying the same summary. Do not force every item into all three roles.
